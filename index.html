Ara Play

Make a game. Publish it in a minute.

Cast your characters in missions, races and endless runs, string up to ten chapters into a campaign with a city to explore between them, then put it on the web with one click. No code, no engine, no install.

Open Ara Play →Read the Ara Play guide →Hosting your game →

Missions

Escape, rescue and clear across five locations, with objectives to reach, download, defend, eliminate, survive, escort, chase and convoy.

Companions and vehicles

Free a companion who follows you to the truck and rides along. 14 drivable vehicles, from a keke to a muscle car.

Combat

Pistols, rifles and grenades, cover, stealth takedowns, snipers, heavies and squad leaders.

A city between chapters

Free roam with traffic, pedestrians, police and a wanted level, side jobs and a car radio.

Arcade races

11 circuits, 13 cars, drifting, nitro, items, ghosts, cups and expert rivals.

Endless runs

11 settings, 7 power-ups including spring boots and a hoverboard, objects to find and armed enemies to shoot.

Campaigns and story

Up to ten chapters in any mix of modes, with branching choices, dialogue, voices and opening and ending films.

Assist and Customize

Describe a game to any AI assistant and paste back a playable campaign. Set its name, How to play and colours.

Publish online

Live on Cloudflare in about a minute with no sign-up, ready to claim into your own free account. Or download it and host it anywhere.

Ara 3D Studio

Direct your characters in short films

Take any character from the builder, cast up to five of them in each scene, and direct each one's performance on a timeline. Add dialogue, sound and music, then export an MP4. Free, in your browser.

Open Studio →Read the Studio guide →

66 locations

Offices, homes, beaches, a neon alley, a highway, a harbour, a military base and a football stadium.

Up to five characters per scene

Your own characters or presets, each with its own timeline of actions, placement and equipment.

216 animations

Talk, dance, sit, fight, shoot from cover, vault, climb, swim and get up again.

Vehicles

Cars, motorbikes, boats, a horse, a tank, a helicopter and a jet, with boarding, driving and crashes.

Action

Fight scenes, sword combos, gunfights from cover and gunshot reactions with blood.

Sound

Typed or recorded dialogue, 20 sound effects and 9 music tracks, mixed into the film.

Cameras and text

Wide, medium and close shots with their own angles, plus subtitles and speech bubbles.

Assist

Describe a film to any AI assistant and paste back a complete, editable project.

MP4 export

720p or 1080p at 30 fps with sound, landscape or portrait. Save and reopen projects.

What you get

Everything between a blank scene and a playable character

Realistic humans

Built on the CC0 MakeHuman dataset: 900+ morph targets, 219 detail sliders, 20 photo-based skins and three detail levels from about 5k to 120k triangles.

Clothing and culture

Mix-and-match tops, bottoms, wrappers, uniforms and 30+ accessories. Kente, aso-oke, ankara, adire, camo and leather, all recolourable.

Hair, beards, tattoos

Cornrows, box braids, a punk mohawk and buzz cuts. Stubble to full beards. Tattoos and earrings. All follow every head and body shape.

Automatic rigging

Mixamo bone names for Unity Humanoid, Unreal and Godot. Game, lite or full skeleton, A- or T-pose, face bones and a weapon socket.

Faces that come alive

Characters blink and glance around. 9 expressions with real teeth and tongue, a talking clip, and lip-sync mouth shapes, exported as blend shapes in GLB and FBX.

216 animations

A motion-captured walk and run, turns, jumps, pickup, press, open door, push, carrying, sitting and lying down, unarmed fighting, emotes, a rifle and pistol shooter set with cover, sword combos, and vehicles: car, bike, motorcycle, horse, canoe, kayak and boat.

Weapons and props

Rifle, pistol, sword and knife on a socket bone; the hands stay on the weapon in every pose. And a bicycle, fitted to every rider, for the cycling animation.

Exports you can trust

Static, rigged, or rigged + animations; one file or one per clip. The export panel lists every file before you download.

Batch command line

Self-host it and generate hundreds of NPCs from a script. Every export carries its settings, so any character can be regenerated.

Get started

Use it online, or own the whole pipeline

The online builder has every feature for making characters by hand. Self-host it for batch generation, offline use and your own garments, hair and animations. Works on Windows, macOS and Linux with Node 20+ and git.

Open the builder → View on GitHub 
# one-time setup
git clone https://github.com/olatunde85/3d-gen.git
cd 3d-gen
npm install
npm run setup-assets
# run it
npm run dev
