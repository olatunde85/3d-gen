01Build

Characters you’d actually ship.

Start from a preset or a blank body and shape everything: body, face, skin, hair, clothes and props. Every character is rigged and animated the moment you export it.

Open the builder →
Realistic humans
The CC0 MakeHuman dataset: 900+ morph targets, 219 detail sliders, 20 photo-based skins and three detail levels.
Clothing and culture
Tops, bottoms, wrappers, uniforms and 30+ accessories in kente, aso-oke, ankara, adire, camo and leather, all recolourable.
Hair, beards, tattoos
Cornrows, box braids, a mohawk and buzz cuts, stubble to full beards, tattoos and earrings, fitted to every head.
Automatic rigging
Mixamo bone names for Unity, Unreal and Godot. Game, lite or full skeletons, A- or T-pose, face bones and a weapon socket.
Faces that come alive
Blinking, 9 expressions with real teeth and tongue, and lip-sync mouth shapes, exported as blend shapes.
216 animations
Motion-captured walks and runs, fights, emotes, sitting, swimming, a shooter set with cover, and riding 14 vehicles.
Weapons and props
Rifles, pistols, swords and knives held properly in every pose, bags to carry and vehicles fitted to each rider.
Exports you can trust
GLB, FBX and OBJ: static, rigged or animated, one file or one per clip. The export panel lists every file first.

02Film

Direct your characters in short films.

Cast up to five characters in a scene and direct each one on a timeline. Add dialogue, sound and music, then export an MP4. Every shot in this reel was made that way.

Open Studio →Studio guide

66 locations
Offices, homes, beaches, a neon alley, a highway, a harbour, a military base and a football stadium.
Five characters a scene
Your own characters or presets, each with its own timeline of actions, placement and equipment.
Vehicles
Cars, motorbikes, boats, a horse, a tank, a helicopter and a jet, with boarding, driving and crashes.
Action
Fight scenes, sword combos, gunfights from cover and reactions when someone is hit.
Sound
Typed or recorded dialogue, 20 sound effects and 9 music tracks, mixed into the film.
Cameras and text
Wide, medium and close shots with their own angles, subtitles and speech bubbles.
Assist
Describe a film to any AI assistant and paste back a complete, editable project.
MP4 export
720p or 1080p at 30 fps with sound, landscape or portrait. Save projects and reopen them.

03Play

Make a game. Publish it in a minute.

Cast your characters in missions, races and endless runs, string up to ten chapters into a campaign, then put it on the web with one click. No code, no engine, no install.

Open Ara Play →Ara Play guideHosting your game

Missions
Escape, rescue and clear across five locations: reach, download, defend, survive, escort, chase and convoy.
Companions and vehicles
Free a companion who follows you and rides along. 14 drivable vehicles, from a keke to a muscle car.
Combat
Pistols, rifles and grenades, cover, stealth takedowns, snipers, heavies and squad leaders.
A city between chapters
Free roam with traffic, pedestrians, police and a wanted level, side jobs and a car radio.
Arcade races
11 circuits, 13 cars, drifting, nitro, items, ghosts, cups and expert rivals.
Endless runs
11 settings and 7 power-ups, including spring boots and a hoverboard.
Campaigns and story
Up to ten chapters with branching choices, dialogue, voices and opening and ending films.
Publish online
Live in about a minute with no sign-up, ready to claim into your own free account, or download it and host it anywhere.
