THE MISSION PLAN

Use the cover.

Press C or tap Crouch to duck behind low cover; press it again to stand. Crouching lowers your sight and hit profile, slows movement and still allows aiming and reloading. It does not make you invisible from the sides or at close range. With Agile movement, use Jump at a parked pickup’s hood or rear tailgate. Rear entry lands inside the bed; face the cab and Jump again to climb onto its roof. Come down to the ground before boarding. Occupied vehicles cannot be climbed.

  1. With Rescue + pickup extraction, free your companion and lead them to the waiting truck. Keep them following and interact at the driver’s door. They walk to the opposite door and board as your passenger; driving waits until both are seated. Keep both doors clear. Stop and interact to alight together. To finish, drive both characters into the extraction ring and stop. With Clear enemies + pickup extraction, defeating the squad unlocks the truck.
  2. In Getaway, find the pickup keys and board at the driver’s door. The checkpoint opens with the keys. W / Up accelerates, S / Down brakes then reverses, A / D steers and Space brakes. On a phone, push the joystick forward/back to accelerate/reverse and sideways to steer, with the Brake button for a quick stop. Stop before E / Interact to alight. The pickup stays where you park it; return to the same door to board again. Drive into the green extraction ring and stop to finish. People and obstacles stop the pickup, and enemies can shoot the driver.
  3. In Clear and extract, defeat every enemy before reaching extraction. The gate starts open. The grey extraction ring turns green when the squad is cleared. Automatic checkpoints save the cleared squad and your equipment. Choose a starting pistol or Find a pistol; Leave no trace is unavailable for this combat mission.
  4. In Rescue and escape, follow the blue companion marker through the open gate. Get close and use Interact to free them. Lead them back to the green extraction marker. Interact near them to wait/follow; both characters must arrive. They stay on the ground when you climb and need a clear route.
  5. In Escape, find the gold key marked on the map. Get close and press E or tap Interact.
  6. Follow the lane to the gate. Use the key to unlock it.
  7. Nearby enemies investigate the gate. Hold Shift or toggle Run to get away.
  8. Containers block sight and movement. Break sight to make the guard search your last position.
  9. Walk over supply boxes to collect health (+25) or ammo (+24 reserve rounds) when needed. Supplies stay available while you are full or unarmed. With Find a pistol, collect the marked pistol in the starting yard. Objective unlock equips it after collecting the key or freeing your companion.
  10. Reach the green extraction ring on the map. Objectives scoring rewards mission progress, speed and fewer retries. Action also rewards defeated enemies. Checkpoints restore earned points, equipment and collected supplies together.
  11. With Agile movement, press Space or tap Jump near cargo. Ara chooses a vault or climb when there is a safe landing. Low steps work automatically; you can walk on cargo tops.
  12. Automatic checkpoints save after collecting the key and unlocking the gate, or after freeing the companion in a rescue. Capture, an alarm or running out of time uses one life. Retry restores completed objectives and gives you 3 protected seconds. Restart mission begins a fresh run.

With grenades equipped, press G or tap Grenades to see the throw arc and blast radius. Point at the ground (or drag on a phone), then press F, click, or tap Throw. Escape / Cancel exits aiming. A grenade explodes 2.6 seconds after release. Cargo blocks blasts; nearby explosions can hurt you and your companion. Explosions raise the alarm in Leave no trace. Checkpoints restore grenade inventory and clear active grenades.

With a pistol, hold right mouse to aim and left click to fire. Q toggles aim, F fires and R reloads. On a phone, use Aim / Fire / Reload; tapping an enemy chooses your target. Cargo and gates block shots. Armed enemies aim before shooting: move or use cover. Losing health uses a life. Firing ends respawn protection. Move with WASD or arrow keys. Drag sideways to rotate the scene. In Third person and Close follow, drag vertically to tilt up or down. Scroll to zoom. On a phone, use the thumbstick and Run button. Press V or tap the camera button to cycle through the views allowed for this game. The pause menu also lets you select a view. Camera rotation and zoom depend on the game setup. The sound button mutes both background music and combat audio. Escape pauses.
