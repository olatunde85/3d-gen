3D characters in one line of code.

Realistic, rigged and animated humans, generated on your users' devices. A three.js library for your web app or game, and a command-line tool for your pipeline. Free and open source, with no server and no account.

What people build with it

Games and crowds

Heroes, enemies and whole streets of NPCs from presets or seeds. Clone one character a hundred times for almost nothing.

Talking characters

Expressions, natural blinking and 14 lip-sync mouth shapes with the names lip-sync tools use. Pair with any voice or AI.

Avatars and try-on

Let users design someone in the builder, then load their share link straight into your app.

Asset pipelines

Generate GLB, FBX or OBJ in CI for Unity, Unreal, Godot or Blender, from small character files kept in Git.

AI agents

Published JSON schemas let assistants write valid characters that the library or CLI builds.

Prototypes

A believable cast for a pitch, a demo or a game jam in minutes, without a 3D artist.

Prefer files? Use the command line.

The same characters as GLB, FBX, OBJ or ZIP, built on your machine. The first run downloads the data once; after that it works offline.

Command-line guide 
# a hero with a rifle, walking and running
$ npx ara3d export --preset "The Soldier" --weapon rifle --animations walk,run
# 200 townsfolk for a crowd
$ npx ara3d export --random 1-200 --detail low --no-animations
# your own character, as a file you can keep in Git
$ npx ara3d new hero && npx ara3d export --params hero.json --formats glb,fbx
