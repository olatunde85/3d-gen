Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

In short: Ara runs in your browser. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking cookies, and the characters, films and games you make stay on your device unless you choose to publish or share them.

This policy covers ara3dgen.com: Ara 3D Gen (the character builder), Ara 3D Studio and Ara Play, together "Ara". Ara is run by Joshua Olatunde ("we", "us").

What stays on your device

Building characters, directing films and making games all happens in your browser. Your work is saved in your browser's local storage, or in files you download. We never receive it. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so download anything you want to keep.

Cookies and browser storage

Ara sets no cookies. To keep your work and settings between visits, it uses your browser's own storage, which stays on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Local storage: your current character, export settings and preferences.
  • Session storage: carries a character between the builder and Studio while you switch.
  • IndexedDB: audio and media you add to films and games.
  • Cache storage: the voice model, so it downloads only once.

Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies, such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance, to tell people from bots. They aren't used for tracking or advertising. Because nothing here tracks you, there's no cookie banner. You can clear all of this at any time in your browser's site settings.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes request details such as your IP address and browser type to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

Features that connect to other services

These only run when you use them.

Publish online (Ara Play)

When you publish a game, its files pass through our publishing service, which runs on Cloudflare and Vercel, to Cloudflare. Cloudflare creates a temporary account for your game, which you can claim as your own within 60 minutes; Cloudflare deletes unclaimed accounts. Our service checks the game and relays it, and stores nothing. Your IP address is used briefly to limit how quickly anyone can publish. Once published, a game is public to anyone with its address. Cloudflare's Terms and Privacy Policy apply to the account and the published game.

Car radio (Ara Play)

The station list comes from Radio Browser, and the audio streams straight from each station. They see your IP address, as with any online radio.

Voices (Ara 3D Studio)

The first time you use generated voices, the speech model downloads from Hugging Face. After that, speech is generated on your device and the text you type is not sent anywhere.

LLM Assist

LLM Assist sends nothing. You copy a prompt into an AI assistant of your choice and paste its answer back; that assistant's own privacy policy applies to what you give it.

Links and shared games

Share buttons and links open other sites, such as GitHub or social networks, whose own policies apply. Games other people publish are hosted on their own Cloudflare accounts, not by us.

Children

Ara is a general creative tool and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version is posted here with a new date.
